Use cases

Ask it like
you'd ask an editor.

Hook breakdowns

Spoken hook, on-screen text and visual of every Reel's first seconds, typed by pattern.

“Break down how these 12 Reels open and which pattern gets the most likes per day.”

Topic sweeps

Web search plus reels_links turns a topic into a list of Reels to study.

“Find Reels about meal-prep for busy parents and summarise the angles they take.”

What creators said

Verbatim quotes with the second they were said, from any of 25 European languages.

“What do these coaches say about pricing? Quote them.”

Visual style audits

Frames show framing, text overlays, subtitles and cut pace across a set of Reels.

“How do these brands use text on screen in the first two seconds?”

Competitor content

Paste a competitor's Reel links and get a structured comparison of message and format.

“Compare these 8 Reels from two competitors: topics, hooks, calls to action.”

Trend formats

Music-only Reels are flagged, so trend-and-text formats separate cleanly from talking ones.

“Which of these Reels rely on a trending sound instead of speech?”

Honest limits

What it can't do
without a login.

No search inside Instagram

Instagram offers logged-out visitors no search, hashtag pages or profile listings. Reels come from your links or from web pages via reels_links.

No Instagram captions

Instagram's own auto-captions are only served to logged-in users, so transcripts come from on-device speech-to-text. It can mishear names; noisy audio lowers accuracy.

Likes, not views

View counts are hidden from logged-out visitors. The server reports likes, likes per day and comment counts.

Rate limits and private Reels

Instagram throttles anonymous lookups; the server spaces them and says when to wait. Private, age-restricted or blocked Reels are skipped with a reason.

Questions.

Does it need my Instagram login?

No. It reads only what Instagram shows any logged-out visitor of a public Reel. No login, cookies or API key, so your account is never involved.

Where does the transcript come from?

From the Reel's audio, transcribed on your machine with Moondream's Parakeet Redux, a 178 MB version of NVIDIA's Parakeet TDT v3. Instagram's own captions are only served to logged-in users. Audio is processed locally and deleted right after.

Which languages work?

The 25 European languages of Parakeet v3, with automatic detection: English, German, French, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Czech, Slovak, Ukrainian, Russian, Swedish, Danish, Finnish, Estonian, Latvian, Lithuanian, Hungarian, Romanian, Bulgarian, Croatian, Slovenian, Greek and Maltese.

How does the agent find Reels on a topic?

Instagram has no logged-out search, so the skill searches the web for pages that embed or list Reels on your topic, such as articles, roundups and newsletters, and reels_links collects the Reels from them. You can always paste your own links.

What does setup install?

--setup creates a private Python environment in ~/.cache/reels-research-mcp with Moondream's Photon runtime (it brings PyTorch, about 1 GB in total) and downloads the 178 MB model. yt-dlp and ffmpeg are needed for Reel data and frames.

Is it affiliated with Instagram or Meta?

No. It is an independent open-source project. Use it in line with Instagram's terms and respect creators' rights when you quote or reuse their content.
