# Writing up Reels research

1. **Answer first:** 3–5 findings with counts ("6 of 14 Reels open with on-screen text before any speech").
2. **The table:** one row per Reel, verbatim spoken hook and on-screen text, cited as `@handle · Reel URL · 0:00`. Sort by likes/day, not raw likes.
3. **Patterns:** group by hook type; 1–2 best examples per group.
4. **Coverage:** pages scanned, Reels found and read, skipped with reasons.

Rules:
- Every quote is verbatim and says its source: transcript (speech-to-text) or frame (on-screen text).
- Numbers (likes, comments, dates) come from tool output.
- Say what the data cannot show: likes are not views (Instagram hides views without login), the sample comes from pages that link Reels rather than from Instagram's own ranking, and speech-to-text can mishear names.
- Write in the user's language; keep quotes in the original language with a translation in brackets when useful.
